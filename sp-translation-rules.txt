Translation Rules Summary for Mastering Cardano (EN)

1. Fidelity: Translations must be faithful and strictly aligned with the original text. No unnecessary paraphrasing or additions.
2. Language & Tone: Translate into formal Mexican Spanish (es-MX).
3. Brand: Always keep "Mastering Cardano" in English as a recognized brand.
4. Formats: Source texts may be in Markdown (.md) or AsciiDoc (.adoc).
5. Do NOT translate:
   - Code blocks (``` in Markdown, or equivalent delimiters in AsciiDoc).
   - File names in images or linked files (e.g., image.png, diagram.adoc, contract.plutus).
6. Preserve formatting and structure:
   - Keep bold, italics, headings, lists, links, references, and anchors intact.
   - Maintain the same order and hierarchy of sections as in the source.
7. Terminology:
   - Use consistent Cardano ecosystem terms (stake pool, EUTXO, Ouroboros, etc.).
   - Always translate 'proofs' as 'pruebas formales' in the context of formal methods and blockchain.
   - Always translate 'property testing' as 'pruebas de propiedades' in the context of formal methods.
   - Keep standard English terms used in the community untranslated, and italicize when appropriate.
8. Output:
   - Deliver translations as downloadable files with the correct extension (.md or .adoc).
   - Keep UTF-8 encoding and preserve line breaks where possible.
9. Quality Control before delivery:
   - Ensure code blocks and file names remain untranslated.
   - Verify grammar, punctuation, and capitalization in titles and subtitles.
   - Confirm structure and formatting match the original.
   - Ensure terminological consistency throughout.

Objective: Provide high-fidelity, technically accurate translations ready for direct integration into the Mastering Cardano repository.
